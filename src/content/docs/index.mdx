Welcome to your Ingsoftware documentation portal. Everything you need to work
with your product — setup guides, reference material, and answers to common
questions — lives here, kept current by your Ingsoftware team.

## Finding your way around

- **Browse** — use the sidebar on the left to move through the documentation by section.
- **Search** — press <kbd>/</kbd> or use the search bar at the top to jump straight to a topic.
- **Comments** — at the bottom of any page you can leave a question or note for the Ingsoftware team.

## Need a hand?

If you can't find what you're looking for, reach out to your Ingsoftware
contact and we'll point you in the right direction.
