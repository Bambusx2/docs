This portal documents how to set up, use, and operate your Ingsoftware
product. This page gives you the big picture; the sidebar breaks everything
down into setup, day-to-day guides, and technical reference.

## What the product does

At a high level, the platform lets your team:

- **Connect** your existing systems and data sources.
- **Configure** workflows and access without writing code.
- **Operate** with confidence through audit logs, monitoring, and support.

## Core concepts

A few terms appear throughout the documentation. Getting comfortable with
these now will make everything else easier to follow.

| Concept | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Workspace** | The top-level container for your organisation's data and settings. |
| **Project** | A scoped area inside a workspace, usually one per team or product line. |
| **Member** | A person with access to a workspace, governed by a role. |
| **Role** | A named set of permissions (for example, *Admin*, *Editor*, *Viewer*). |
| **Integration** | A connection to an external system that syncs data in or out. |

## How the pieces fit together

A workspace contains one or more projects. Each project holds your data and
the integrations that feed it, and members are granted access through roles.

:::note
You only need one workspace to get started. Most customers add projects as
their usage grows rather than planning the whole structure up front.
:::
